QuestionsFAQ
What do I need to send?+

The MLS link or address, a few phone photos, the price and what stands out. That is enough for us to write the plan.

When do I get the plan?+

By your next morning, Vancouver time. Send it in the evening and the plan is in your inbox when you start your day.

Do I need a camera?+

No. A phone filming vertically is enough. The shot list tells you where to stand and how long to hold each shot.

How fast is the edited reel?+

First cut in 48–72 business hours after we receive your clips. Two rounds of revisions are included.

Can I request in Korean?+

Yes. Send the request and get the plan in English or Korean — by email or KakaoTalk.

Is there a monthly option?+

Yes. A monthly plan covers four reels and two carousels, with a reel plan for each. Tell us how often you post and we'll quote it.
