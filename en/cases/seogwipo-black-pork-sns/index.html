Black Pork Restaurant Social Media Management

Nammaene · Black pork restaurant in Seogwipo · 2 locations · monthly

Since September 2026 · ongoing (monthly)

Brief

The restaurant's strengths and tone were buried under trend-meme reels, and the feed did not hold together.

What we did

Built a three-format rotation around the concept "A city inside the countryside" and directed in-store reels and the owner's on-camera episodes.

Output

27-page content plan · monthly schedule · reels · save-worthy carousels · staff shooting cards · season-based production system

Problem

The profile and brand story were solid, but the reels on the feed were mostly trend memes, so the restaurant's strengths and tone did not carry through.

Analysis

Reviewed the existing feed, profile and in-store assets, and separated what cannot be changed (the old market alley, the taste of the meat) from what the owner curated personally (the drinks line-up, the interior).

Planning

Set the concept "A city inside the countryside" and wrote a 27-page plan and monthly schedule that rotates three formats: table poster, rhythm of cooking, and dining snapshots.

Production

Produced cinematic, drink-pairing and staff-tip reels plus save-worthy carousels from in-store footage, with one standard for color, location sound, subtitle position and end card.

Shoot direction

Designed additional shoots in advance as a plan and script broken down by scene, time and line, filmed the owner's episodes, and made shooting cards so staff can film on their own.

Production system

From November, content is designed in four levels — season → series → episode → cut — so it accumulates every month, and one shoot is reused across Instagram, Xiaohongshu, Danggeun and Naver. Store information uses only values confirmed by the owner.

Deliverables

  • Brand content plan (27 pages)
  • Monthly production plan · schedule
  • Finished reels · 2 save-worthy carousels
  • Additional shoot plan · shooting script
  • Edited reels featuring the owner
  • 3 staff shooting cards
  • Season-based production system document (28 pages) · Nov–Dec schedule
