Sales Marketing for a Detached House Development

Livingshow · Detached house development in Jeju · sales and interior company · monthly

Since October 2026 · ongoing (monthly)

Brief

Ad spend got buried among other local property ads, and listing information was scattered across blog, Instagram and Danggeun.

What we did

Set the direction as content that shows the site as it is plus a dedicated link page, then ran drone filming and a lesson on editing the website.

Output

13-page sales marketing proposal · drone stills and video · dedicated link page · website review deck

Problem

Even with ad spend, the listings were buried among property ads in the same area, and with information scattered across blog, Instagram and Danggeun there was no single place to show an interested buyer.

Analysis

Reviewed the site, competing developments and the channels the company already had, and listed strengths other developers lack: interiors finished by the company itself, and views of Hallasan and the sea.

Proposal

Instead of competing on ad spend, set the direction as "content that shows the site as it is + a dedicated link page" and wrote a proposal covering the first-month launch and the monthly routine that follows.

Production

Filmed the whole development, Hallasan, the sea direction and the nearby roads by drone, and designed a dedicated link page that gathers the scattered listing information on one page.

Training

Taught the owner how to change photos and text on the website, and summarized it as a review deck to follow. Posting and inquiry handling continue together on a monthly basis.

Deliverables

  • Sales marketing proposal (13 pages)
  • Drone stills · video of the development
  • Dedicated link page design
  • Website editing review deck
