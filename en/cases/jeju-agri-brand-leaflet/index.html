Agricultural Brand Certification · Leaflet Design

Zeus · Agricultural company in Jeju · 2 print pieces · about 1 week

November 2026 (about 1 week)

Brief

The procedure for the new cultivation certification, how to read the number, and the effect of the material were mixed together on one sheet.

What we did

Split the story across the folded panels in the order farmers ask about it, and made the management service leaflet in four color options.

Output

Print-ready PDF of the tri-fold certification leaflet · four color options for the orchard management leaflet · folded-view image set

Problem

A new scheme issues a certification number to farms that use the genuine material, but the existing handout mixed the certification procedure, how to read the number and the effect of the material on one sheet.

Analysis

Based on the existing leaflet and the owner's explanation, organized the order of what farmers want to know: what it does for them → whether it can be trusted → how to apply.

Planning

The certification leaflet runs front cover → first opening → fully opened → back, with one story per fold. Each panel is self-contained so that no photo or box crosses a fold line.

Production

Organized and placed the certification mark, the seal and the per-plot certification number system. The orchard management service leaflet keeps the same structure in four color options (orange, teal, navy, light green) for the owner to choose from.

Delivery

Delivered a print-ready PDF with 3 mm bleed together with images of one sheet, two sheets and the folded view, ready for the print shop and online posting. Contact details are hidden in the portfolio images.

Deliverables

  • Tyvek cultivation certification tri-fold leaflet (print-ready PDF)
  • Orchard management tri-fold leaflet in four color options
  • Image set of one sheet · two sheets · folded view
