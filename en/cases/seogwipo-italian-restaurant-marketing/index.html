Italian Restaurant Marketing Audit · Planning

Perlo · Restaurant in Andeok, Seogwipo · audit report · content plan

September 2026 (audit report · proposal · content plan)

Brief

Food quality and recognition were already high, but there were almost no in-house video assets to carry that trust forward.

What we did

Audited ratings and reviews on 9 channels and competing restaurants, and linked review keywords to reels and pre-visit guide content.

Output

Marketing audit report · video production proposal · 8-page content plan · plan for five 15-second reels

Problem

Food quality and recognition were already high, but there were almost no in-house video assets to carry that trust forward.

Analysis

Checked Instagram, Dining Code, Tripadvisor, Polle, Siksin, Catch Table, Naver Place, YouTube and Google Maps directly, tabulated ratings, reviews and activity by channel, and graded each as good / average / lacking. Compared positioning against local competitors, extracted the positive and negative keywords that repeat in reviews, and mapped the gaps by content type, summarized as a grade table with one-line conclusions.

Planning

Turned the audit into a content plan in this order: current state → key tasks (now → after) → structure of the main film by section → a series of five 15-second reels → content for online sales. Positive keywords became reel topics, and negative keywords became pre-visit guide content.

Proposal

Organized the owner's goals and delivered a production proposal defining target, channels and selling points, together with a week-by-week production schedule.

Deliverables

  • Online marketing audit report
  • Video production proposal (goals · target · selling points · schedule)
  • Video content production plan (8 pages)
  • Reels series plan (15 seconds × 5)
