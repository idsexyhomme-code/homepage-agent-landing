Citrus Brand Store · Detail Page · Instagram

Zeus · Daldarin · Citrus brand of a Jeju agricultural company · 18-panel detail page · Instagram

November 2026

Brief

The brand had real strengths — Tyvek cultivation certification, sorted sweetness, same-day shipping — but none of it showed up as one brand on the sales screens or social accounts.

What we did

Defined the Daldarin logo, colors and tagline, then built the store screens, an 18-panel Smart Store detail page and the Instagram account on the same standard.

Output

Store main screen (PC · mobile) · 18-panel detail page · 5 Instagram posts · 7 highlights

Problem

Tyvek cultivation certification, sorting at 13 Brix and above, and same-day shipping for orders paid by 10 a.m. were real advantages, but they were scattered across channels and did not read as one brand.

Brand

Set "Sweetness you choose, Daldarin" as the core line and applied the citrus-tree symbol, orange and navy palette and typefaces identically across the store, detail page and Instagram.

Store screens

Built PC and mobile main screens that run in order: the brand line and a "13 Brix" badge, the three points (certification · sorted sweetness · same-day shipping), a seasonal calendar for mandarins and hybrid citrus, and the 3 · 5 · 10kg options.

Detail page

18 panels in the order Tyvek cultivation → origin → certification → certification-number sticker → sorted sweetness → same-day shipping → the actual 5kg box → seasonal calendar → storage → FAQ → contact, answering questions before customers ask. Box photos were shot with real shipping boxes.

Instagram

Wrote the @daldarin.jeju profile, 7 highlights (Daldarin · Season · How to choose · Certification · Storage · Ordering · Contact) and the first 5 posts (2 reels · brand journal · seasonal update · how-to-choose card), with the profile leading to a Naver search for "Daldarin".

Labeling

Every screen carries the same notes: sweetness is a sorting standard that varies by harvest and fruit, and the cultivation certification is not a national quality certification.

Deliverables

  • Brand standard (logo · colors · tagline)
  • Store main screen (PC · mobile)
  • 18-panel Smart Store detail page
  • Instagram profile · 7 highlights · 5 posts
  • Brand reel · landscape video
