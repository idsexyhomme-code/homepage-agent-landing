Detail Pages · Print

Produce · Food Product Detail Pages

5 Smart Store products · restaurant brand identity

1–2 weeks per product (after receiving product materials and photos)

Brief

Produce looks the same in photos, so the first screen did not show why this seller's product is the one to buy.

What we did

Found the point where buyers hesitate for each item and structured the page as first screen > proof photos > option comparison > delivery guide.

Output

5 detail pages (mandarin · Red Hyang · kohlrabi · kabocha · chili powder) · option comparison table · restaurant brand manual

Problem

Produce looks alike in photos, so it was hard for a buyer to see on the first screen why they should buy from this seller.

Analysis

Found the point of hesitation for each product first: sweetness and origin for mandarins, gift packaging for Red Hyang, "what it is and how to eat it" for kohlrabi, size and options for kabocha, and origin and processing for chili powder.

Planning

Arranged sections to read as first screen → selling points → proof photos → option comparison → delivery and storage guide, and shortened the copy so one mobile screen carries one message.

Production

Used large photos of the farm, harvest and packing, and gave each item its own color and typeface so products stay distinct within the same store. For the restaurant brand, logo, color and application examples were compiled into a brand manual so the same standard applies to detail pages, signage and packaging.

Operations

Detail pages are handed over with editable source files so they can be changed as soon as prices or options change. Prices and contact details are hidden in the portfolio images.

Deliverables

  • 5 detail pages (mandarin · Red Hyang · kohlrabi · kabocha · chili powder)
  • Option comparison table · delivery · storage guide sections
  • Restaurant brand identity manual
