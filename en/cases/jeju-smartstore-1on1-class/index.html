One-on-One Smart Store Practical Class

Staff member at a mandarin distribution company · 6 on-site sessions

October – November 2026 (6-session course)

Brief

The store existed, but old products were mixed in and the person in charge did not know how to list and edit products alone.

What we did

Worked through to opening with real products in the order brand > product structure > listing > detail page > search > delivery.

Output

17 class slides · review deck per session · instructor notes · opening checklist

Problem

The store already existed, but old products and names were mixed in, and the person in charge did not know how to list and edit products on their own.

Diagnosis

In the first session we looked at the current store together, separated what to fix (store information, existing products) from what to create (brand, product pages), set an opening date and broke it into a three-stage schedule.

Curriculum

Proceeded in the order brand → product structure (three option levels) → price and fees → product listing → detail page planning → three elements of search → delivery settings → sales channels. We took apart competitors' first screens together and decided the first line for our product, such as "same-day dispatch + measured sweetness".

Review materials

Made a "Read this first" review deck for each session, with one action per slide so the lesson can be repeated as is, and listed what to do before the next session.

Result

The person in charge can now list and edit products and set up delivery on their own, and we are building new product detail pages together in the same way.

Deliverables

  • Class slides (17)
  • "Read this first" review deck per session
  • Instructor notes (rules · fees)
  • Opening checklist
