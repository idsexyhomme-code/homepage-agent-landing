Reels · Carousels for an Overseas Realtor

Korean realtor in Vancouver, Canada · remote · monthly

Since October 2026 · ongoing (monthly remote production)

Brief

There was plenty of listing footage, but it needed a structure that lets Korean-speaking clients grasp the space at a glance and move on to an inquiry.

What we did

Built a 45-second reel format that opens with the question clients ask first, and a monthly market report carousel.

Output

Listing tour reels (condo · townhouse) · reel template · monthly market report carousel (Korean · English)

Problem

There was plenty of listing footage, but it needed a structure that lets Korean-speaking clients understand the space at a glance and continue to an inquiry.

Analysis

Organized each listing's area, room layout, view, amenities, nearby parks and routes, and took the question Korean-speaking clients ask first ("Isn't a one-bedroom too small?") as the opening hook.

Planning

Built a 45-second vertical reel structure in the order hook → space → view → amenities → neighborhood → call to inquire, reflecting the realtor's own script.

Production

Edited with a fixed template: listing summary at the top, an English headline with Korean handwritten-style subtitles in two tiers, and brand mark at the bottom. Tempo and subtitle placement were refined over several rounds of feedback.

Carousel

Each month the local real estate board's data is explained at the level of Korean-speaking clients and published as a carousel in the order "this month's market in one line → key numbers → buyers · sellers → invitation to consult".

Operations

From the next listing on, only the information and clips change and the same format is used, on a monthly basis. Listing prices are hidden in the portfolio images.

Deliverables

  • Listing tour reels (45-second vertical) — condo · townhouse
  • Reel template (listing summary · two-tier subtitles · brand mark)
  • Monthly market report carousel (Korean · English)
