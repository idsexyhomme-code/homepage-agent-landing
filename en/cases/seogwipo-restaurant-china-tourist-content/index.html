Social Media

Platform-by-Platform Content Set for Overseas Visitors

Nammaene · Black pork restaurant in Seogwipo · for visitors from China and Taiwan

November 2026 (November posting set)

Brief

There was almost no store information on Xiaohongshu and Dianping, the apps travelers actually search.

What we did

Organized the menu, location and family-visit information travelers ask about first, and built a one-month set shaped to each platform's format.

Output

8 Xiaohongshu notes · Traditional Chinese Instagram · 18-photo Dianping album · Chinese menu · Danggeun and Naver updates

Problem

Posting only on Instagram left almost no store information on the apps travelers actually search (Xiaohongshu and Dazhong Dianping).

Analysis

Re-read the meeting notes and store materials and listed what travelers ask first: what to order, which location fits, whether children are welcome, and what the evening atmosphere is like.

Planning

On Xiaohongshu, the notes are numbered as a "Nammaene Jeju Notes" series so they read as one set on the account's first screen. Informational notes (menu, location, visiting with children) go up first, with atmosphere notes mixed in afterwards.

Production

Each note has its own cover photo and layout. From the same shoot we produced posts for a Traditional Chinese Instagram sub-account, an 18-photo Dianping album, 6 recommended dishes, a Chinese menu, Danggeun and Naver Place updates, and two weeks of Instagram stories. Every photo was taken in the actual restaurant.

Operations

Post copy and a posting calendar for each platform are delivered together, and the owner reviews everything on a preview page that opens on a phone.

Deliverables

  • 8 Xiaohongshu notes (cover · inner pages · post copy)
  • Posts for the Traditional Chinese Instagram sub-account
  • Dazhong Dianping album of 18 photos · 6 recommended dishes · Chinese menu
  • Danggeun · Naver Place update posts
  • 2 weeks of Instagram stories (10)
  • Posting calendar · phone preview page
