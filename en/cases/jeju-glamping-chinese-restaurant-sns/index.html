Social Media

Glamping Site and Chinese Restaurant, Managed Together

Jayeonin Glamping · Chinese restaurant · Glamping site + Chinese restaurant in Jeju City · monthly

Since September 2026 · ongoing (monthly)

Brief

Both businesses posted in bursts and then went quiet, and the information guests ask about first was scattered.

What we did

Audited Instagram and Naver, built one operating calendar for both businesses, and designed 3 Xiaohongshu notes for Chinese guests staying at nearby hotels.

Output

17-slide monthly management deck · 10-page note plan · Chinese ordering and arrival cards · response tracking sheet

Problem

Both businesses had accounts, but posts came in bursts and then stopped, and the information guests ask first — stay conditions, opening details — was scattered across places.

Analysis

Checked Instagram and Naver Place directly and summarized posting flow, gap periods, exposure of old accounts, and the state of reviews and updates. A priority guest was set for each business; for the tangsuyuk restaurant, Chinese travelers staying at the large hotels nearby.

Planning

Built an operating calendar that puts both businesses' weekly production and posting items and the shared shoot day in a single table. Designed 3 Xiaohongshu notes that Chinese travelers save and open again in the restaurant — a Chinese ordering card, a menu comparison video and an arrival guide — down to card layout and video storyboard.

Post copy

Wrote the Chinese title, body and hashtags for each note, and set the principle of stating that the account belongs to the business, with no exaggeration or bait wording.

Operations

Fixed the order of confirm → shoot → post, and set a standard for recording views, saves, inquiries and visits separately each month.

Deliverables

  • Monthly management proposal deck for two businesses (17 slides)
  • Xiaohongshu ordering · visit note plan (10 pages)
  • Channel audit (Instagram · Naver)
  • First-month production · posting calendar
  • Layout for Chinese ordering card · arrival guide card
  • Post copy for 3 notes (Chinese + Korean meaning)
  • Tracking sheet for responses · inquiries · visits
