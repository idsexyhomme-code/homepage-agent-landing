YouTube Channel Management

Gaebyeok Seonwon · Seon center channel in Jeju · Shorts · long-form · card news · monthly

Since September 2026 · ongoing (monthly)

Brief

Shorts went up steadily, but views were stuck in one range and there was no standard for which topics get a response.

What we did

Analyzed all 193 Shorts to set the direction, and edited 2-minute Shorts, Q&A long-form videos, a thumbnail series and card news.

Output

Channel audit meeting document · edited Shorts · 4 long-form videos with thumbnails · 3 card news sets · upload copy

Problem

Shorts went up steadily, but views were stuck in a certain range and there was no standard for which topics get a response.

Audit

Collected view counts for all 193 Shorts and 10 long-form videos from the public page, and summarized what worked, what did not and ideas for next topics as a meeting document.

Shorts editing

From long recordings, picked only the segment that answers one question, cut it to about 2 minutes and unified the title bar and lower subtitles. On request, a version with the original picture and lower subtitles only was delivered as well.

Long-form · thumbnails

Split the Q&A recording into 4 videos under 7 minutes each, and made thumbnails that keep the series frame while changing hand gesture, color and text position per episode so each is recognizable at a glance. Source loudness was measured first so the audio does not clip.

Card news

Explained easily confused terms as 4-card sets placed between Shorts to give the channel a study-channel feel, in a form that can be reused on the blog and Danggeun.

Operations

Upload copy (title, description, chapters) is delivered with each video, and everything is delivered the same day via Google Drive, email and group chat.

Deliverables

  • Channel audit meeting document (all 193 Shorts)
  • Edited Shorts of about 2 minutes (title bar · lower-subtitle versions)
  • 4 Q&A long-form videos · thumbnail series · upload copy
  • 3 card news sets (4 cards each)
