FAQFAQ
How long does production take?+

Usually 1–4 weeks depending on length and scope. We confirm your materials, then give an exact schedule first.

How is pricing decided?+

We don't post fixed prices; after checking your materials and scope, we reply with a tailored quote. Tell us your budget range and we'll organize what's possible and what to leave out.

What about commercial usage rights?+

Delivered videos can be used for ads, websites, social and more within the scope agreed at the consult. If music, stock or AI-generated assets have usage limits, we confirm those before the contract.

Can you make AI short-form without a shoot?+

Yes. Product photos, existing footage, a logo and key lines are enough to build Reels and Shorts AI short-form. If materials are short, we'll guide you on the photos needed.

Are revisions possible?+

Revisions are included within the agreed scope. We lock tone, copy and structure first so big revisions don't come up.

How do we start?+

Just send the result you want, what you have, and your preferred timeline — we'll reply with what's possible and the next step. Pre-contract consults are no-pressure.

Do you work outside Jeju?+

Websites, video and content are produced online nationwide and worldwide. On-site filming and drone are arranged by schedule and region.

Can you handle edits & upkeep after launch?+

Yes. We keep helping with copy and image edits and light upkeep after launch. The scope of upkeep is agreed during the consult.
